# Proposed publisher decisions for ChessBuddy terms

These recommendations are reflected in terms-draft.md and remain proposals until the publisher confirms them. They are drafting assistance, not a finding that the terms are enforceable in every country. Jurisdiction-specific wording should be reviewed locally before public use.

| Item | Proposed choice | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience | Users eligible for ChatGPT, with guardian permission wherever required | Avoids inventing a universal minimum age or excluding otherwise eligible users without a decision |
| Acceptable use | Lawful chess study; no unauthorized access, overload, evasion, malware, or inputs the user cannot provide | Matches a free anonymous chess app without importing unrelated rules |
| Changes and suspension | May change or discontinue the free service; reasonable notice where practicable or required, urgent restrictions for security/legal needs | Describes operational flexibility while preserving mandatory rights |
| Accuracy and availability | Educational guidance and best-effort availability; no guaranteed playing strength, clock precision, or durable storage | Matches implemented limitations |
| Liability | Preserve mandatory rights; no automatic zero-dollar cap, blanket waiver, broad indemnity, or arbitration requirement | Avoids pretending one aggressive clause is appropriate worldwide |
| Law and disputes | Confirm publisher's country/state; default to no exclusive forum pending tailored review | Country targeting and timezone do not establish governing law |
| Effective date | Date the approved terms are actually published | Avoids backdating an unpublished draft |

## Awaiting facts/choices
- Operating country remains unconfirmed. Worldwide availability is the distribution choice, not an operating jurisdiction. The draft currently uses country-neutral wording without an exclusive forum.
- Audience confirmed: ChatGPT-eligible users with parent/guardian permission wherever required.
- Whether a local reviewer recommends a specific governing-law/forum or liability clause.

No live terms page has been published, and no platform attestation has been made. The existing policy and terms drafts are review materials rather than completed submission URLs.
