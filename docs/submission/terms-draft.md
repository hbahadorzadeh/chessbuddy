# ChessBuddy terms — draft for publisher review

Status: unpublished draft. Publisher: Hamed Bahadorzadeh. Contact: h.bahadorzadeh@gmail.com.

## Service
ChessBuddy offers free chess practice, elapsed timers, position-aware authored teaching material, and replay of supplied chess records inside supported ChatGPT experiences. Use is also subject to the host platform's eligibility and terms.

## Scope and limitations
ChessBuddy currently has no paid purchases, rated multiplayer service, or engine evaluation. Authored opening suggestions and one-move checks are educational aids, not certified best moves or a guarantee of playing strength. Imported historical metadata is user-supplied and not independently authenticated.

The service uses best-effort Vercel app memory. Positions, replays, or synchronization can become unavailable before their configured seven-day expiry. A stable UUID identifies a game but does not make it durable. Timings include elapsed time away from the app and host response latency; recovered anonymous timings are not competition-grade or tamper-proof.

## User inputs
Provide only chess records and other content that you are entitled to use. Do not submit secrets, confidential personal information, or content that abuses the service or violates applicable law or host platform rules. Do not publish active game identifiers.
