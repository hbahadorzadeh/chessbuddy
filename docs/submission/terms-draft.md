## 5. Educational guidance and timers

Guidance is based on authored teaching notes, standard opening continuations, and limited legality/tactical checks. It is not a certified best move, a full engine evaluation, or a promise of improvement, rating, or competitive results. Check important analysis independently.

Timers measure elapsed time, including time away and host response latency. Recovered historical timings may be unavailable or approximate. The app is not a competition-grade or tamper-proof clock.

## 6. Availability, storage, and recovery

ChessBuddy is provided on an "as available" basis. The publisher does not promise uninterrupted availability, permanent storage, or that every position or reply can be recovered.

Live games use bounded server memory with a seven-day logical expiry from the last committed move. Replays expire seven days after import. Vercel instance loss or capacity eviction can remove them sooner. A stable game UUID does not make the record durable. Where supported, browser or host state may help restore the displayed history, but this is not a backup guarantee.

Keep your own copy of any chess record you want to retain. Details of data processing and retention belong in the published privacy policy.
